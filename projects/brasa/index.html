BRASA

Spec work — not affiliated with any real brand. An invented hot sauce brand in three heat levels: naming, wordmark, label system, product photography, three posters, social pieces, street posters and two 10-second spots (vertical and horizontal).

Discipline
Visual design
Year
2026
Role
Naming, identity and label design, campaign concept and copywriting, art direction, AI image and video generation, layout of the pieces
Deliverables
Name, wordmark and heat-level system, Three labels as a series, E-commerce packshot, Three posters in Spanish and English, Table photography and gift box, Social pieces (1:1, 9:16, 16:9), Street posters, 10-second spots in 9:16 and 16:9
Tools
Google Flow · Nano Banana 2.1 · Omni 1.2 Flash · Claude

The brief

A range of three craft hot sauces that has to stand out on a shelf full of skulls, cartoon flames and threatening names. The brief is treated as a full launch: name, labels, online shop imagery, posters, social pieces and a spot for each channel.

The idea

The heat explains itself, with no drawings: three names that climb in intensity (CHISPA, LLAMA, INCENDIO: spark, flame, blaze), three dots that fill up and three colours. The campaign speaks just as plainly: “Hot, just a bit”, “Hot, properly”, “Hot. Full stop”. One warning signs off every piece: “You were warned”.

AI production

The labels were designed first as vector artwork and given to the AI as a reference, so the bottles wear the real label rather than one made up by the model. Every photograph and both spots start from that same range image. AI does not write small text well: on the packshot the two thin lines were corrected by hand. Headlines, posters and the wall mock-up were laid out on top of the images afterwards.

Design language

The wordmark

BRASA is set in an extra-bold condensed sans, rotated and running the full height of the label. On a narrow bottle, setting it vertically is how the name becomes the biggest thing on the shelf.

Three levels

Each sauce has its own colour for label, cap and backdrop: yellow for CHISPA, red for LLAMA, black for INCENDIO. The three dots in the corner fill up with the level, so the heat reads from a distance, without reading.

Posters and pieces

The three posters are tone on tone: each bottle on its own colour, its ingredients beside it and the headline above in the wordmark’s typeface. Together they work as a triptych; apart, each one introduces a level. The warning “You were warned” closes every piece at the bottom right.

Colour palette

Red is the brand colour: the wordmark, the headlines and the middle sauce. Yellow and charcoal mark the other two levels. Cream is the paper: the backdrop for the range, the text on red and the inside of the box.

Result

Concept project. Spec work: BRASA is an invented brand and product, not affiliated with any real company. It shows a complete launch, from the name and the label to the spots, with the same product in every piece and a three-level system that needs no explanation.
