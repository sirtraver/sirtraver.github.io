Before we start

What kinds of projects can I propose?

Visual identity, rebranding, packaging and visual communication. Share the context and scope so I can assess how I can help.

What information do you need to get started?

Your brand name, what you want to achieve, the deliverables and your target date. Visual references and an indicative budget help define the proposal.

How are budgets and timelines agreed?

They depend on scope, deliverables and availability. The first contact is an enquiry: work begins once the proposal and its terms have been agreed.

Is every portfolio project a real commission?

The portfolio also includes personal explorations. COLORSPLASH, Olmedo Clásico and Autoescuela OASIS are labelled “Concept project”; their applications show design proposals. Back to the projects →
