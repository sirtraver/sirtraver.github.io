Services

Three ways to work together, from the logo to the spot. Each proposal is built from your brief.

  1. 01

    Brand identity

    For
    New brands, or brands that are changing before a launch.
    What it solves
    Every piece is improvised and the brand looks different in each place.
    Usual deliverables
    • Logo and its versions
    • Colour palette and typefaces
    • Graphic system: patterns, stamps, rules
    • Usage guide
    Examples
    KUDAKE! Japan BurgersCOLORSPLASHOlmedo Clásico
  2. 02

    Packaging and product

    For
    Brands with a product or a range to launch.
    What it solves
    The products don’t read as one family, on the shelf or in the online shop.
    Usual deliverables
    • Labels and packaging
    • Variants of the range
    • Packshots for the online shop
    • Product and lifestyle photography
    Examples
    BRASAOYE
  3. 03

    Campaign and spots

    For
    Brands launching or advertising a product.
    What it solves
    They need pieces ready to publish that keep the same identity in every format.
    Usual deliverables
    • Key visual
    • Adaptations for social media (1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9)
    • Posters
    • Short spots in 9:16 and 16:9
    Examples
    SOLFABRASAOYE

How it works

Budgets depend on scope: tell me what you need and I’ll send you a proposal.

  1. 01

    Brief

    You tell me about the brand, the product and what you need. The brief on the Contact page takes a minute.

  2. 02

    Proposal

    I reply with a proposal: scope, deliverables, timeline and terms. Work begins once you accept it.

  3. 03

    Production

    I share progress at the agreed points so you can review it before the final pieces.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    You receive the final files in the agreed formats, ready to use.
