Visual design · 2026 · Concept project

SOLFA

Spec work — not affiliated with any real brand. A launch campaign for an invented lemon soda: product images, social pieces and a 15-second spot, all created with AI and art-directed like a photo and film production.

Discipline
Visual design
Year
2026
Role
Concept, art direction, AI image and video generation, layout of the pieces
Deliverables
E-commerce packshot, Product and lifestyle photography, Campaign key visual, Social pieces (1:1, 9:16, 16:9), 15-second spot
Tools
Higgsfield · Nano Banana 2 · Seedance 2.0 · Claude

The brief

A sparkling lemon soda entering a shelf crowded with lemon brands. The brief is treated like a real launch: a can that reads from a distance, product images that work in an online shop, and a campaign that adapts to any social format without losing the idea.

The idea

SOLFA plays on two readings: sol, the sun, and solfa, the musical scale. The red circle on the can is both the six o’clock sun and a musical note. That leads to the line “Turn up the sun”: opening the can turns up the volume of the afternoon. Cans on plinths of rising height turn it into a scale, do, re, mi.

AI production

A reference image of the can was fixed first. Every other image and the spot were generated from it, so the label, colour and proportions stay the same from piece to piece. The campaign copy was laid out on top of the images afterwards; the AI was never asked to write anything except the label itself.

Design language

The can

A slim 330 ml can in matte butter yellow. The name runs vertically in a very heavy geometric sans and fills almost the full height: it reads from a distance and leaves the top free for the red circle and the flavour.

Art direction

Hard late-afternoon light, long crisp shadows and single-colour backdrops. The packshot on white meets online shop standards; the terrace and rooftop scenes carry the same light into a Mediterranean setting.

System of pieces

One layout for every format: black headline top left, brand line below it and the product as the hero. On the story the headline turns white against the sky.

Colour palette

Yellow is both the product and the backdrop; red is the sun and appears only once per piece; black carries the name and the headlines.

Result

Concept project. Spec work: SOLFA is an invented brand, not affiliated with any real company. It shows how a complete product campaign (packshot, photography, social pieces and a spot) can be produced with AI while keeping the product identical across every piece.
