COLORSPLASH

Spec work. Identity, packaging and launch for an invented paint brand in four colours.

Discipline
Visual identity
Year
2026
Role
Visual identity, packaging and retail; strategy and concept for the launch campaign, channel plan, art direction, AI image and video generation, layout of the pieces
Deliverables
Identity and monogram, Tins and box, Retail display, “Paint it” campaign, 10-second spot
Tools
Affinity · ChatGPT · Higgsfield · Nano Banana 2 · Google Flow · Omni 1.2 Flash · Claude

The challenge

The paint aisle is a wall of identical tins, and first-time painters end up choosing white.

The idea

A CS monogram and four colours, CMYK, organise the whole range. The campaign turns hesitation into a command: “Paint it.”

What was made

Identity, tins and box, retail display, packshot, “Paint it” banner and posts, and a 10-second spot.

Design language

Colour palette

  • Cyan#009FC5
  • Magenta#E51658
  • Yellow#FFC21F
  • Black#141414

Result

Concept project. COLORSPLASH is an invented brand. The full case is available on request.
