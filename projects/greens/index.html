Visual identity · 2024

Greens

A visual identity for a coffee brand. An approachable wordmark, a leaf and a mascot connect the mark with its product and the small rituals of a coffee shop.

Discipline
Visual identity
Year
2024
Tools
Affinity · ChatGPT

The project

Greens develops an identity that moves from packaging to menus and from communication to the coffee-shop setting. The presentation brings together a wordmark, colour variants, a mascot and print applications. A small set of devices connects the different pieces.

The idea

A leaf completes the name and makes the brand’s natural reference visible. The mascot turns a coffee cup into a character, introducing a more expressive register. Both share a curved visual language, allowing a restrained brand presence to alternate with more approachable communication.

Applications

Menus, coffee bags, cups, T-shirts, paper bags and posters show the system on different surfaces. The name usually sits in a clean, recognisable position, with the mascot as a complementary device. The colour range connects the applications.

Design language

Typography and symbol

The name uses rounded shapes, a heavy weight and open counters. The leaf extends the wordmark’s silhouette rather than introducing a separate language. The combination creates a compact reading and keeps the mark visible on small packaging and in compositions with generous empty space.

Colour and materials

Green, beige and mustard create a warm range around the presentation of coffee. Green carries identification, while light tones and paper materials provide the ground. Contrast variants and colour reversals explore the system without changing the wordmark’s structure.

Mascot and composition

The personified cup adds eyes, a mouth and a more gestural outline. It contrasts with the quieter packaging and stationery compositions. A shared colour range allows the mascot to bring expression where needed, while the wordmark handles more restrained applications.

Result

An identity system connecting name, symbol and character. The applications explore how one personality can carry through product, service and communication surfaces.
