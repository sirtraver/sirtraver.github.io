KUDAKE! Japan Burgers

Spec work. Identity, packaging and opening campaign for a smash burger restaurant with a Japanese soul.

Discipline
Visual identity
Year
2026
Role
Visual identity, packaging and applications; strategy and concept for the opening campaign, channel plan, art direction, AI image and video generation, layout of the pieces
Deliverables
Identity and stamp, Packaging, Restaurant and merchandise, “砕け!” campaign, 8-second spot
Tools
Affinity · ChatGPT · Higgsfield · Nano Banana 2 · Seedance 2.0 · Claude

The challenge

The city is full of new smash burger places, and on delivery apps every photo looks the same.

The idea

The name is a shout: 砕け! (smash it!). A blue and orange stamp turns it into the brand and into the campaign’s smash moment.

What was made

Stamp and identity, packaging, restaurant and merchandise, social and delivery pieces, a “SMASH.” banner and an 8-second vertical spot.

Design language

Colour palette

  • Rice Cream#F4EBDD60% of use
  • Electric Cobalt#1F4FFF30% of use
  • Chili Orange#FF5A2A10% of use

Result

Concept project. KUDAKE! is an invented brand. The full case is available on request.
