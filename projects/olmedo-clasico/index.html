Olmedo Clásico

An independent concept identity for a classical theatre festival. Condensed typography, burgundy and ivory connect posters, signage and tickets.

The project

An independent concept identity for a classical theatre festival. The presentation includes branding, posters, a simulated festival setting and tickets.

Visual system

Burgundy and ivory unify the presentation. Tall, condensed typography structures the titles, while the initials OC form a repeating motif on the margins and tickets.

Applications

Engraving-style illustration and typographic composition carry through from the posters to the venue graphics and tickets. These are concept applications, independent of a commission from the festival.

Colour palette

  • Burgundy#722F43
  • Ivory#F2E8D8

Select an image to explore the details.

Result

Concept project. It explores how two colours, a condensed typeface and an engraving-style illustration can give a classical theatre festival a contemporary, recognisable image, from poster to ticket.
