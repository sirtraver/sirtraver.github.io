I work on visual identity and brand communication, from logos to their digital and printed applications. My experience also includes product photography and visual content editing.

I’m currently studying an Advanced Vocational Qualification in Marketing & Advertising at UFV, expanding my understanding of brands, their audiences and how they communicate.
