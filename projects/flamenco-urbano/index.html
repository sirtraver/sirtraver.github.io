Flamenco Urbano

Spec work. A flamenco-inspired streetwear brand, launched in Tokyo.

Discipline
Visual identity
Year
2026
Role
Strategy, naming, identity, campaign and art direction
Deliverables
Naming and bilingual identity, Labels and bag, Drop 01 and lookbook, Billboard and B1 posters, Pop-up and product page
Tools
Affinity · ChatGPT

The challenge

Flamenco has a big following in Japan, but it lives in academies and tablaos. The task: launch Spanish streetwear without it looking like a costume.

The idea

From the tablao to the street: polka dot, ruffle and fan in everyday clothes. An FU monogram, the name in katakana and a carmine dot.

What was made

Naming, bilingual identity, labels and bag, Drop 01, lookbook, billboard and B1 posters, a Harajuku pop-up and product page.

Design language

  • Carmine#C61F2E15% of use

Result

Concept project: Flamenco Urbano is an invented brand. The full case is available on request.
