Olmedo Clásico

An independent concept for a classical theatre festival. An editorial identity connecting the visual memory of engraving with contemporary typographic composition.

Discipline
Visual identity
Year
2026
Deliverables
Visual identity, Posters, Tickets, Programme
Tools
Affinity · ChatGPT

The challenge

A classical theatre festival needs each play to stand out while all of them read as one programme.

The idea

Two eras: engraving-style illustrations and a tall, narrow typeface. Two colours, burgundy and ivory, and the OC monogram.

What was made

Wordmark and monogram, four posters, tickets, a programme and a simulation of the festival on site.

Design language

Colour palette

  • Burgundy#722F43
  • Ivory#F2E8D8

Result

Concept project. It explores how two colours, a condensed typeface and an engraving-style illustration can give a classical theatre festival a contemporary, recognisable image, from poster to ticket.
