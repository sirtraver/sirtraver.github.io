The idea

The name is split into three stacked syllables inside a rounded rectangle. The result is compact, reads at a glance and holds up from an avatar to a poster.

02

System

A single typeface, Cubano, and a palette reduced to black, white and grey (#000000, #FFFFFF, #777777). Positive, negative and grey versions cover every background.
