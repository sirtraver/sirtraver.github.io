Flamenco Urbano

Spanish roots, Japanese scene. An identity for a flamenco-inspired streetwear brand designed for the Japanese market.

Discipline
Visual identity
Year
2026
Deliverables
Visual identity, Retail, Merchandise, Streetwear, Outdoor
Tools
Affinity · ChatGPT

The challenge

Take a flamenco-inspired streetwear brand to Japan, where flamenco culture has deep roots. The identity had to speak to that audience and work equally well on a storefront, a garment or a billboard.

The idea

A high-contrast serif FU monogram sits alongside the name in katakana, フラメンコ・ウルバノ. A carmine dot joins both parts and recurs throughout the system.

System

Four logo versions, on bone, charcoal and carmine, plus a monogram for small sizes. The same logic runs through retail, merchandise, garments and outdoor.

Colour palette

  • Bone#EDE3D550% of use
  • Charcoal#11111135% of use
  • Carmine#C61F2E15% of use

Select an image to explore the details.

Result

A bilingual identity that reads in Madrid and in Tokyo: monogram, katakana name, four logo versions and a three-colour palette applied to the shop, the collection, merchandise and an outdoor campaign.
