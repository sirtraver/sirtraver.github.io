About / SIRTRAVER

JAVIER
TRAVER.

Visual designer based in Madrid, Spain.

I work at the intersection of graphic design, marketing and advertising. I’m interested in how an idea becomes a clear visual identity, a useful piece of communication or a resource someone can put to work.

I also explore practical uses of artificial intelligence in everyday creative work: organizing information, asking better questions and developing ideas with intention.

SIRTRAVER brings together my design portfolio and digital resources. The work lives on Behance; the downloads are managed through Gumroad.
