BRASA

Spec work — not affiliated with any real brand. A full launch for an invented hot sauce brand in three heat levels: from strategy and naming to labels, campaign, website and two spots.

Discipline
Visual design
Year
2026
Role
Strategy, naming, identity, campaign and art direction
Deliverables
Strategy and naming, Identity and guidelines, Labels and box, Campaign: posters, street panels and social, Web, email and banners, Two 10-second spots
Tools
Google Flow · Nano Banana 2.1 · Omni 1.2 Flash · Claude

The challenge

A small hot sauce kitchen wants to move beyond farmers’ markets. On the shelf, sauces are either traditional and explain nothing, or extreme and scary. None of them says what the buyer wants to know: how hot it is.

The idea

Make heat the brand system: three names that climb (CHISPA, LLAMA, INCENDIO: spark, flame, blaze), three dots and three colours. The campaign is just as plain: “Hot, just a bit”, “Hot, properly”, “Hot. Full stop”. And it warns once: “You were warned”.

What was made

Strategy, range naming, identity and guidelines, labels, gift box, posters and street panels, social, web, email, banners and two 10-second spots. The labels were designed first and used as the AI reference, so the product is identical in every piece.

Design language

The wordmark

BRASA runs vertically, full height: on a narrow bottle it is how the name becomes the biggest thing on the shelf.

Three levels

One background colour and three dots that fill with the level: the heat reads from a distance, without reading.

Campaign system

The same grid everywhere: the line on top, the product on its colour and “You were warned” bottom right.

Colour palette

Red for the brand; yellow and charcoal for the other two levels; cream as the paper.

Result

Concept project. Spec work: BRASA is an invented brand. The full case, with strategy, guidelines and campaign plan, is available on request.
