Visual identity · 2025

sirtraver

My own brand: a typographic block in three syllables, SIR · TRA · VER, in black and white, working as logo, stamp and signature.

Discipline
Visual identity
Year
2025
Deliverables
Visual identity, Stationery, Posters
Tools
Affinity · ChatGPT

The project

An identity for signing my own work and connecting pieces across different disciplines. The mark needs to sit beside colourful projects with different visual languages. A compact form and a neutral palette allow it to accompany the work without competing with it.

The idea

SIR · TRA · VER divides the name into three stacked units. This turns a long word into a recognisable block and links the name with a simple graphic gesture. A rounded container holds the three lines together, allowing the mark to act as a stamp, avatar or signature.

Applications

Business cards, stationery and poster compositions use the same block as their identifying element. Personal information follows a secondary hierarchy around it. The system connects small formats with larger pieces without needing a different logo for each use.

Design language

Typography

Cubano Regular forms the main block. Thick strokes, open curves and rounded terminals create a compact silhouette. The name works as a shape as well as text, with a clear relationship between its three stacked lines.

Composition and scale

The rounded rectangle contains the rhythm of the three syllables. Inner margins separate the letters from the edge and keep the block from becoming too dense. Across applications, the contrast between stamp and empty space gives the mark presence without filling the whole surface.

Identity variants

Positive, negative and grey versions retain the same structure. What changes is the figure–ground relationship, rather than the shape of the mark. This restraint helps the identity coexist with different project images and maintains consistency across stationery, screen and posters.

Colour palette

Black and white provide the main contrast. Grey acts as an intermediate version and a supporting ground. By limiting the identity to these tones, colour can come from each project while the signature connects different pieces of work.

  • Black#000000
  • White#FFFFFF
  • Grey#777777

Result

A personal brand that signs all my work: a single typographic block in positive, negative and grey versions, applied to cards, stationery and posters.
