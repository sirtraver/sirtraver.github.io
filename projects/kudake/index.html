KUDAKE! Japan Burgers

Smash burgers with a Japanese soul. An identity for a burger chain that brings the American neo-diner into a contemporary format, with the kanji 砕け! (“smash it!”) as its stamp.

Discipline
Visual identity
Year
2026
Deliverables
Visual identity, Interior, Merchandise, Packaging, Posters
Tools
Affinity · ChatGPT

The challenge

Give an identity to KUDAKE!, a Japanese smash burger chain built for global expansion: a brand as direct as its name and legible in any city.

The idea

Kudake means “smash it!” in Japanese, exactly what happens to the patty on the griddle. The kanji 砕け! leads as a stamp, and KUDAKE! JAPAN BURGERS translates it for everyone.

System

Electric blue and orange on a cream ground. Four graphic devices carry every piece: the repeated stamp as a pattern, the block shout, the checkerboard from the restaurant tiles and the triple rule taken from the logo.

Colour palette

  • Rice Cream#F4EBDD60% of use
  • Electric Cobalt#1F4FFF30% of use
  • Chili Orange#FF5A2A10% of use

Result

A complete system ready to grow: stamp, logo, a palette with usage proportions, typography and four graphic devices applied the same way across the restaurant, packaging, merchandise and the street.
