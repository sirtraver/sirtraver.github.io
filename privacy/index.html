Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Data controller

Javier Traver. Contact: javier.traver.v@me.com.

What data is collected

If you sign up for the resources launch notice: your email address, the language of the page and the date and time of your consent.

If you write to me by email (directly or with the brief on the Contact page): the data you include in your message, usually your name, email address, brand and project details. The brief is prepared in your browser and nothing is sent until you send the email yourself.

When you select a language, this website saves the functional cookie sirtraver_lang for up to six months to remember your choice. It contains only es or en and is not used for advertising or tracking. On your first visit, the browser language is used if no choice has been saved. The server may process technical data (such as IP address and browser) temporarily to deliver the website securely and prevent abuse.

Purpose

Launch notice: to email you when the sirtraver digital resources launch. Project enquiries: to answer your enquiry and, where applicable, prepare a proposal.

Legal basis

Launch notice: your consent, given with the checkbox of the form (art. 6.1.a GDPR); you can withdraw it at any time. Project enquiries: answering a request you make before a possible agreement (art. 6.1.b GDPR).

Retention

Launch notice: until it is sent or until you ask for deletion. Contact messages: for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and at most two years.

Recipients

Your data is not sold. Technical hosting and storage providers process it to operate this website, hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When email delivery is enabled, the email provider also processes the data needed to deliver the message.

Your rights

You can access, rectify or delete your data, object to or restrict its processing, and request its portability by writing to javier.traver.v@me.com. You can also file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Form provider

Launch notice sign-ups are received through Web3Forms, which forwards them by email to the owner and acts as data processor.

Visit statistics

Aggregated, anonymous visit statistics (pages viewed, referring site, country, device type and clicks on buttons and links) are collected with Umami Cloud, a cookieless tool. It does not store cookies on your device, does not identify you and does not track you across sites.

External content

Fonts, portraits, project covers and all project gallery images are served from this website. Product images on the Resources page are loaded from Gumroad. Links to other services (Behance, Gumroad, Instagram, Threads, LinkedIn) are subject to their own privacy policies.
