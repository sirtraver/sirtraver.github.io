Visual design · 2019

WOBBLE

A product design proposal that turns sitting into an experience of balance. WOBBLE explores a curved-base seat, accompanied by a visual identity with rounded, unstable forms.

Discipline
Visual design
Year
2019

The project

The exercise starts with an everyday object, a seat, and proposes a more active relationship with it. The boards present the idea, form, product views and identity. The focus is on connecting the proposed behaviour with the form and visual experience of the product.

The idea

The roly-poly reference brings movement into the object’s base. Its curved surface proposes a seat that can tilt and return to position. This idea of balance also shapes the name: letters appear to move independently within a word that retains its unity.

Visual development

Views, construction lines and tilting sequences explain the proposal from different angles. Renders introduce colour variations and make the volume recognisable. The identity supports the presentation without replacing the explanation of the product.

Design language

Form and balance

Continuous surfaces and rounded edges define the silhouette. The contrast between the open back and more enclosed base makes the seat read as one volume. Tilting diagrams make visible the movement principle behind the concept.

Typography

Gotham Rounded Book brings an even stroke and rounded terminals. Rotated and displaced letters create an unstable reading line in the logo, connecting the name with the object. Typography supports the concept rather than only identifying it.

Colour and presentation

Turquoise and white connect the boards, while renders explore the product in brighter colours. Clean backgrounds separate the silhouette from its setting. Alternating volume views and line drawings explains both the object’s character and the logic of its form.

Result

A proposal connecting product form and visual identity through the same idea of balance. The presentation follows the concept from silhouette and views through to its typographic interpretation.
