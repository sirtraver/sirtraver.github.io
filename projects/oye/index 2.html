OYE

Spec work — not affiliated with any real brand. Launch of an invented pebble-shaped portable speaker: product photography, three colourways, social pieces, a product page and two 15-second spots (vertical and horizontal), all created with AI.

Discipline
Visual design
Year
2026
Role
Concept product design, campaign concept, art direction, AI image and video generation, layout of the pieces and the product page
Deliverables
Product design and three colourways, E-commerce packshot, Lifestyle photography, Key visual in Spanish and English, Social pieces (1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9), Product page mockup, 15-second spots in 9:16 and 16:9
Tools
Higgsfield · Nano Banana 2 · Seedance 2.0 · Claude

The brief

A portable speaker competing in a category full of plastic cylinders with lights. The brief is treated as a full launch: designing the object and its colours, the online shop imagery, the social campaign, the product page and a spot for each channel.

The idea

OYE is shaped like a river pebble and blends in wherever you leave it: on a shelf, among beach stones, by a lake. It goes unnoticed until it plays. The name itself is a call for attention (oye means “hey, listen” in Spanish), and the line comes straight from it: “Hear this”.

AI production

The product was first designed in two variants and one was chosen as the reference. Every image and both spots were generated from it, so the shape, fabric and details stay the same from piece to piece. One packshot came out with a plinth it should not have had and was redone. The headlines and the product page were laid out on top of the images afterwards; the AI was never asked to write text.

Design language

The object

A slightly asymmetric river pebble wrapped in stone-grey knit fabric. An ultramarine silicone band at the base and a single button in the same blue: all the product’s colour sits in those two places. The wordmark is lowercase and debossed, the same colour as the fabric.

Three colourways

The range repeats the same rule: a neutral fabric and one bright colour on the band and button. Stone and ultramarine, charcoal and orange, sand and sage. Each version stands apart without changing the shape.

Typography and pieces

Headlines use a geometric sans with rounded details, close to the soft forms of the product and its wordmark. On the key visual the headline is centred in white on blue; on the other pieces it sits top left in ink. The product page follows the same scheme: a large headline, one sentence and a single blue button.

Colour palette

Stone grey is the product and off-white is the space. Ultramarine is the only bright colour: it marks the button, the band and the key visual backdrop. The other two versions swap that blue for orange or sage.

Result

Concept project. Spec work: OYE is an invented brand and product, not affiliated with any real company. It shows a complete launch produced with AI, from product design to spots, with the same object in every piece and each format adapted to its channel.
