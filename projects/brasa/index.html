BRASA

Spec work. An invented hot sauce brand with three heat levels, from the name to the campaign.

Discipline
Visual design
Year
2026
Role
Strategy, naming, identity, campaign and art direction
Deliverables
Naming and identity, Labels and box, Campaign and social, Web, email and banners, Two spots
Tools
Google Flow · Nano Banana 2.1 · Omni 1.2 Flash · Claude

The challenge

On the shelf, no sauce says what buyers want to know: how hot it is.

The idea

Heat as the system: three names that climb (CHISPA, LLAMA, INCENDIO: spark, flame, blaze), three dots and three colours. And one warning: “You were warned”.

What was made

Naming, identity, labels, gift box, posters, street panels, social, web, email, banners and two spots.

Design language

Colour palette

  • Spark yellow#F5B83A20% of use
  • Charcoal#1A161420% of use
  • Cream#F6ECD620% of use

Result

Concept project: BRASA is an invented brand. The full case is available on request.
