Flamenco Urbano

Spanish roots, Japanese scene. An identity for a flamenco-inspired streetwear brand designed for the Japanese market.

Discipline
Visual identity
Year
2026
Deliverables
Visual identity, Retail, Merchandise, Streetwear, Outdoor
Tools
Affinity · ChatGPT

The starting point

The project connects a flamenco-inspired streetwear brand with a presentation aimed at a Japanese context. It seeks a shared language between different references: expressive clothing, an editorial brand character and a name presented in two writing systems.

The idea

The FU monogram concentrates the identity, with the katakana name supporting its reading. A carmine dot acts as a link: it sits beside the mark and becomes a graphic device across the applications. This simplicity is paired with fashion images that bring greater intensity and detail.

Applications

The presentation moves through storefronts, garments, accessories, product arrangements and outdoor visuals. The monogram allows a compact presence on objects, while the full name and fashion imagery lead at larger scales. The pieces share one visual world, from a label to an urban scene.

Design language

Typography

Didot Bold brings contrast between thick and thin strokes and an editorial reference associated with fashion. Regular and Italic variants extend that register into supporting text. M PLUS Rounded 1c supports the Japanese name and secondary levels. The contrast separates brand signature from information without breaking the whole.

Monogram and graphic devices

The FU initials interact through overlap and changes in scale. The carmine dot provides a solid counterpoint to the serif’s thin strokes. It extends into circles and repetitions that act as backgrounds, patterns or accents. The identity alternates restrained surfaces with more visually dense applications.

Art direction

The fashion imagery shown in the mockups combines expressive garment volumes, warm light and night-time urban scenes. This treatment connects collection details with the character of the identity. Product compositions use accessories and textiles to repeat the brand’s colour and material world.

Colour palette

Bone and charcoal create light and dark grounds that give the typography room. Carmine is reserved for the dot and stronger accents. Used sparingly, a small detail retains its presence even beside complex images.

  • Bone#EDE3D550% of use
  • Charcoal#11111135% of use
  • Carmine#C61F2E15% of use

Result

An identity pairing a restrained typographic signature with expressive art direction. The monogram, name, carmine dot and palette connect fashion, product, retail and outdoor applications.
