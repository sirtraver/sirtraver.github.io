Visual design · 2026 · Concept project

OYE

Spec work. An invented pebble-shaped portable speaker, from the object to the launch.

Discipline
Visual design
Year
2026
Role
Strategy, naming, object design, identity, campaign and art direction
Deliverables
Object and three colourways, Naming and identity, Box with sleeve, Campaign and social, Web, email, banners and two spots
Tools
Higgsfield · Nano Banana 2 · Seedance 2.0 · Claude

The challenge

A speaker spends more time switched off in plain sight than playing, and most look like gadgets.

The idea

A speaker that passes for a stone: grey fabric and a single coloured button. The campaign makes a game of finding it: “One of these stones plays music”.

What was made

The object in three colourways, naming, identity, box, street panels, social, web, email, banners and two spots.

Design language

Colour palette

  • Stone grey#9C989045% of use
  • Ultramarine#2B3FC425% of use
  • Off-white#F3F1EC20% of use
  • Ink#1D1D1D10% of use

Result

Concept project: OYE is an invented brand. The full case is available on request.
