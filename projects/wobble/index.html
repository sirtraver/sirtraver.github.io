WOBBLE

A product design proposal that turns sitting into an experience of balance. WOBBLE explores a curved-base seat, accompanied by a visual identity with rounded, unstable forms.

Discipline
Visual design
Year
2019

The project

A seat that turns sitting down into a balancing experience, with an identity that shares its shape.

The idea

Like a roly-poly toy, the curved base lets the seat tilt and return to its position. The letters of the name sway too.

What was made

Seat form and views, a tilt diagram, wordmark, the Gotham Rounded typeface, a turquoise and white palette and a colour range.

Design language

Colour palette

  • Turquoise#28B8BC
  • White#FFFFFF

Result

A proposal connecting product form and visual identity through the same idea of balance. The presentation follows the concept from silhouette and views through to its typographic interpretation.
