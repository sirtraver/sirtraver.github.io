Visual design · 2026 · Concept project

SOLFA

Spec work. An invented sparkling lemon soda in three flavours, from the can to the launch.

Discipline
Visual design
Year
2026
Role
Strategy, naming, identity, can and range, campaign and art direction
Deliverables
Naming and identity, Can and three flavours, Campaign, street panels and social, Terrace kit, Web, email, banners and spot
Tools
Higgsfield · Nano Banana 2 · Seedance 2.0 · Claude

The challenge

Every terrace fridge holds five near-identical lemon sodas. Nobody asks for one by name.

The idea

SOLFA, sun and sol-fa: opening the can turns up the evening sun. “Turn up the sun.” The range plays like a scale: do, re, mi.

What was made

Naming, identity, the can in three flavours, street panels where the sun rises, social, terrace kit, web, email, banners and a spot.

Result

Concept project: SOLFA is an invented brand. The full case is available on request.
