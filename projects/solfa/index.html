Visual design · 2026 · Concept project

SOLFA

Spec work — not affiliated with any real brand. A full launch for an invented lemon soda: from the name and can to a three-flavour range, the campaign, terraces, website and spot.

Discipline
Visual design
Year
2026
Role
Strategy, naming, identity, can and range, campaign and art direction
Deliverables
Strategy and naming, Identity and guidelines, Can and three flavours, Campaign: KV, street panels and social, Terrace kit, Web, email, banners and spot
Tools
Higgsfield · Nano Banana 2 · Seedance 2.0 · Claude

The challenge

A small Mediterranean maker has a good sparkling lemon, but every terrace fridge holds five near-identical lemon sodas. Nobody asks for one by name.

The idea

SOLFA, sun and sol-fa: the can is the six o’clock sun and opening it turns up the evening. “Turn up the sun.” The range plays like a scale: do, re, mi.

What was made

Strategy, naming, identity and guidelines, the can and three flavours, KV, social, street panels where the sun rises, coasters and a table card, web, email, banners and a 15-second spot. Every piece starts from one reference can.

Design language

The can

A single-colour can, the name upright and a red circle: the sun and a musical note.

Art direction

Hard six o’clock light, long shadows and single-colour or sky backdrops.

System of pieces

Black headline top left and a single red sun per piece.

Colour palette

One can colour per flavour; red is always the sun.

Result

Concept project. Spec work: SOLFA is an invented brand. The full case, with strategy, guidelines and campaign plan, is available on request.
