Autoescuela OASIS

The project

A concept identity and communication system for a driving school. The presentation applies the brand to a vehicle, social media, printed material and a website concept.

Visual system

Mint green and electric blue are anchored by a dark tone. A rounded logotype is paired with clear, legible typography and the campaign line “Sin drama. Con carnet.”.

Applications

The car graphics, vertical stories, learner plate and desk materials share the same colours and typography. The website concept brings that identity into a digital interface.
