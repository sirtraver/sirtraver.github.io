COLORSPLASH

A concept identity for a paint brand: a geometric monogram, four colours and a system connecting packaging, communication and retail space.

The project

A concept identity for a paint brand. The presentation follows the brand through its packaging, communication and retail applications.

Visual system

A monogram built from the initials CS is paired with cyan, magenta, yellow and black. The name and colour codes follow a shared structure across the four paint tins.

Applications

Paint tins, a presentation box, communication pieces and a store display show the system at different scales. The campaign is built around the message “Paint it”.

Colour palette

  • Cyan#009FC5
  • Magenta#E51658
  • Yellow#FFC21F
  • Black#141414

Select an image to explore the details.

Result

Concept project. It shows how one monogram and four colours are enough to organise an entire product range: the same system works on the can, the box, the campaign and the in-store display.
