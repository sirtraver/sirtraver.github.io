Autoescuela OASIS

A concept identity and communication system for a driving school. “Sin drama. Con carnet.” turns learning to drive into an approachable, direct and visually recognisable promise.

Discipline
Visual identity
Year
2026
Deliverables
Visual identity, Vehicle graphics, Social media, Print materials, Website concept
Tools
Affinity · ChatGPT

The project

The proposal explores a distinctive voice for a driving school across very different surfaces: a moving car, a social story, course materials and a website. Rather than relying only on traffic symbols, the identity starts with the feeling it aims to communicate: support and less tension while learning.

The idea

The name OASIS and the line “Sin drama. Con carnet.” share a calm but active tone. A short, memorable phrase connects the brand with learning to drive. Communication combines this approachability with bold headlines, giving the proposal energy on the car, in the street and on screen.

Applications

The branded car takes the identity onto a surface that is part of the service itself. The campaign adapts to posters and vertical formats; the learner plate and print materials place the brand in everyday use. The website concept brings these elements into a digital presentation.

Design language

Typography

Tilt Warp gives large messages volume, curves and a slight distortion. Atkinson Hyperlegible provides a quieter register for supporting text. The pairing separates the expressive voice from information: one attracts attention, while the other helps readers move clearly through the content.

Mark and hierarchy

OASIS leads the logo, with the smaller “Autoescuela” descriptor clarifying the activity. Broad text blocks and changes in scale organise the compositions without dividing attention between too many elements. In campaign visuals, the message and car share the lead, connected by the mark.

Imagery and formats

Car scenes and mockups place the identity in contexts related to learning and mobility. Vertical formats retain the headline, vehicle and colour contrast while adapting their relationship to the available space. The interface concept uses the same resources to maintain continuity with the campaign.

Colour palette

Mint green supports the proposal’s approachable tone; the dark shade provides structure and legibility. Electric blue adds a more active accent to headlines and details. Their distribution keeps the friendly character from becoming visually weak.

  • Mint green#A8F0CC
  • Dark#18252B
  • Electric blue#263DD8

Result

Concept project. It imagines a driving school with a friendly, drama-free tone: one clear message, “No drama. Just your licence.”, and a system that stays consistent from the car to social media and the website.
