KUDAKE! Japan Burgers

Smash burgers with a Japanese soul. An identity that turns the energy of the name KUDAKE! into a stamp, a graphic language and a recognisable brand experience.

Discipline
Visual identity
Year
2026
Deliverables
Visual identity, Interior, Merchandise, Packaging, Posters
Tools
Affinity · ChatGPT

The starting point

The project brings together two references: the boldness of a smash burger restaurant and the character of Japanese graphics. The identity connects both without relying on a different illustration for every item. A compact mark anchors the system, organising everything from packaging to the restaurant façade.

The idea

KUDAKE! is treated as a shout: short, direct and connected to pressing the burger onto the griddle. The expression 砕け! takes the first level of the stamp, while the Latin name and JAPAN BURGERS descriptor complete the reading. The name itself becomes the main communication device.

Applications

The stamp changes scale and repeats across paper, cups, boxes, clothing and posters. Checkerboard surfaces and lettering extend the same language into the interior. The applications show how an identity can accompany the whole brand experience while remaining recognisable across materials and formats.

Design language

Typography and voice

Archivo Black gives headlines weight and an immediate presence. M PLUS Rounded 1c introduces rounded forms and connects Japanese lettering with Latin text. The hierarchy contrasts short, large messages with quieter supporting information: the brand’s tone comes first, followed by the detail.

Stamp and composition

A rounded frame contains the name, making the logo a unit that can be placed and repeated easily. A dividing rule organises the reading levels. Outside the stamp, blocks of type, checkerboards and triple rules add rhythm without competing with the mark. Repetition handles both large surfaces and small items with the same elements.

Image and materials

The mockups place the identity in a restaurant setting: food, paper, cardboard, uniforms and interior surfaces. Warm textures and the presence of the product contrast with the stamp’s geometry. Imagery supports the graphic system by showing how it behaves on everyday objects and in everyday situations.

Colour palette

Rice Cream provides a warm ground. Electric Cobalt structures the identity, while Chili Orange concentrates the accents. One light tone and two vivid colours preserve the brand’s character across communication and packaging.

  • Rice Cream#F4EBDD60% of use
  • Electric Cobalt#1F4FFF30% of use
  • Chili Orange#FF5A2A10% of use

Result

An identity built around a stamp and a concise set of rules. The same language connects the product, packaging, clothing and interior, making KUDAKE! recognisable as the scale of the application changes.
